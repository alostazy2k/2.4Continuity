1Continuity at a pointDefinition 2.4.1
Definition 2.4.1Continuity at a number $a$
Assume that $f(x)$ is defined on some open interval containing the number $a$. Then the function $f$ is continuous at $a$ if $\lim\limits_{x\to a}f(x)=f(a)$. This means that:
  1. $f(a)$ is defined,
  2. $\lim\limits_{x\to a}f(x)$ exists, and
  3. $\lim\limits_{x\to a}f(x)=f(a)$.
Remark 2.4.1Substitution principle
If $f$ is continuous at $x=a$, then $\lim\limits_{x\to a}f(x)=f(a)$. Therefore, direct substitution is valid because continuity is known, not as a guess.
التعريف بشروطه الثلاثة، ثم ضغطة تُظهر Remark 2.4.1 (التعويض المباشر مبرَّر بالاتصال).

2Discontinuity taxonomyDefinition 2.4.2
Definition 2.4.2Classification of discontinuities at $x=c$
Let $f$ be a real-valued function defined for all $x$ sufficiently close to $c$ with $x\neq c$. Assume that $f$ is discontinuous at $x=c$. When they exist, define the one-sided limits $$\lim_{x\to c^-}f(x)=M \quad\text{or}\quad \lim_{x\to c^+}f(x)=L.$$

(The limits $M$ and $L$ may be finite real numbers, $-\infty$ or $+\infty$.)

Then the discontinuity of $f$ at $c$ is classified as follows:
  1. Removable: $M$ and $L$ exist, and $M=L=k$, but either $f(c)$ is not defined or $f(c)\neq k$.
  2. Jump: $M$ and $L$ exist, and $M\neq L$.
  3. Infinite: $\lim f(x)$ goes to $-\infty$ or $+\infty$ as $x\to c^-$ or $x\to c^+$ (vertical asymptote).
  4. Oscillatory: $f(x)$ remains bounded near $c$, but $\lim\limits_{x\to c}f(x)$ does not exist due to oscillation.
الأنواع الأربعة: Removable · Jump · Infinite · Oscillatory. الشريحة التالية تعرض كل نوع بالرسم.

Repairing a removable discontinuityRedefine each of the following functions to be continuous at the indicated number, if available:
a. $f(x)=\dfrac{x^{2}-36}{x-6}$ at $x=6$

  3. The new definition of the function is: $$g(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{x^{2}-36}{x-6}, & x\neq 6\\[8pt] 12, & x=6\end{cases}$$

Example 4
Forcing the pieces to agreeDetermine the value of $k$ such that the function $f(x)=\begin{cases}kx+1, & x<2\\ x^{2}-1, & x\ge 2\end{cases}$ is continuous at $x=2$.
  1. To make $f$ continuous at $x=2$, we must have $$\lim_{x\to 2^-}f(x)=\lim_{x\to 2^+}f(x)=f(2).$$
  2. Compute the one-sided limits as $x\to 2$: $$\lim_{x\to 2^-}f(x)=\lim_{x\to 2^-}(kx+1)=2k+1,\qquad \lim_{x\to 2^+}f(x)=\lim_{x\to 2^+}(x^{2}-1)=3.$$
  3. For $\lim\limits_{x\to 2}f(x)$ to exist, these must be equal: $$2k+1=3 \;\Longrightarrow\; k=1.$$
Answer $f$ is continuous at $x=2$ if and only if $k=\mathbf{1}$.
مساواة النهايتين: 2k + 1 = 3 ← k = 1.

3Continuity on intervalsDefinition 2.4.3 · Definition 2.4.4
Definition 2.4.3Continuity on an open interval
Let $f$ be a real-valued function defined on $(a,b)$. The function $f$ is continuous on $(a,b)$ if $f$ is continuous at every number $c$ such that $a<c<b$.
Definition 2.4.4One-sided continuity at endpoints of $[a,b]$
Let $f$ be defined on $[a,b]$.
  1. Right-continuity at $a$: $f$ is continuous from the right at $a$ if $\lim\limits_{x\to a^+}f(x)=f(a)$, where $x$ approaches $a$ using values $x\in(a,b]$.
  2. Left-continuity at $b$: $f$ is continuous from the left at $b$ if $\lim\limits_{x\to b^-}f(x)=f(b)$, where $x$ approaches $b$ using values $x\in[a,b)$.
الاتصال على فترة مفتوحة، ثم ضغطة تُظهر الاتصال من جهة واحدة عند طرفي [a, b].
عدد الضغطات في الشريحة: 1.
3Continuity on intervalsDefinition 2.4.5
Definition 2.4.5Continuity on a closed interval
Let $f$ be defined on $[a,b]$. The function $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$ if it is continuous on $(a,b)$, continuous from the right at $a$, and continuous from the left at $b$.
الفترة المغلقة = التعريفان السابقان معاً: متصلة على (a, b)، ومن اليمين عند a، ومن اليسار عند b.
4The continuity toolkitTheorem 2.4.1
Theorem 2.4.1Algebra of continuous functions
If $f$ and $g$ are continuous functions at $x=a$, then:
  1. $f+g$ is continuous at $x=a$.
  2. $f-g$ is continuous at $x=a$.
  3. $f\cdot g$ is continuous at $x=a$.
  4. $\dfrac{f}{g}$ is continuous at $x=a$, provided $g(a)\neq 0$.
  5. $k\cdot f$ is continuous at $x=a$, where $k\in\mathbb{R}$.
جبر الدوال المتصلة عند نقطة: خمس خواص.
4The continuity toolkitTheorem 2.4.2
Theorem 2.4.2Finite algebra of continuous functions
Let $f_1,f_2,\dots,f_n$ be functions that are continuous at $x=a$. Then:
  1. Finite sums and differences: $F(x)=f_1(x)+f_2(x)+\dots+f_n(x)$ is continuous at $x=a$.
  2. Finite products: $P(x)=f_1(x)f_2(x)\cdots f_n(x)$ is continuous at $x=a$.
  3. Finite quotients: if $g_1,g_2,\dots,g_m$ are continuous at $x=a$ and $g_1(a)g_2(a)\cdots g_m(a)\neq 0$, then $$Q(x)=\frac{f_1(x)f_2(x)\dots f_n(x)}{g_1(x)g_2(x)\dots g_m(x)}$$ is continuous at $x=a$.
التعميم لعدد منتهٍ من الدوال: مجاميع، حواصل ضرب، خوارج قسمة.
4The continuity toolkitCorollary 2.4.3 · Lemma 2.4.4 · Corollary 2.4.5
Corollary 2.4.3Interval version
If $f$ and $g$ are continuous functions on an interval $I$, then $f\pm g$ and $fg$ are continuous on $I$. Moreover, $\dfrac{f}{g}$ is continuous on $I$ provided $g(x)\neq 0$ for all $x\in I$.
Lemma 2.4.4Power functions and polynomials
  1. For each integer $n\ge 0$, the function $f(x)=x^{n}$ is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$.
  2. Every polynomial is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$.
Corollary 2.4.5Rational functions
If $r(x)=\dfrac{p(x)}{q(x)}$ where $p$ and $q$ are polynomials, then $r$ is continuous on its domain.
نسخة الفترات وكثيرات الحدود، ثم ضغطة تُظهر Corollary 2.4.5 (الدوال الكسرية) تمهيداً لـ Example 5.

Lemma 2.4.6Absolute value
The function $f(x)=|x|$ is continuous for every $a\in\mathbb{R}$.
Lemma 2.4.7Square root
The function $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ is continuous on its domain $[0,\infty)$.

4The continuity toolkitRemark 2.4.2
Remark 2.4.2Continuous "standard functions" on their domains
  • Trigonometric functions are continuous on their domains.
  • The absolute value function $|x|$ is continuous for all $x\in\mathbb{R}$.
  • The square root function $\sqrt{x}$ is continuous on its domain $[0,\infty)$.
Consequently, to discuss continuity of $\sqrt{u(x)}$, we must first consider only points where $u(x)\ge 0$ (so the square root is defined).
الدوال القياسية المتصلة على مجالاتها. الجملة الأخيرة (u(x) ≥ 0) هي مفتاح Example 6.

4The continuity toolkitTheorem 2.4.9
Theorem 2.4.9Passing the limit through a continuous outer function
Let $\lim g(x)$ stand for one of the limits $$\lim_{x\to a}g(x),\;\lim_{x\to a^+}g(x),\;\lim_{x\to a^-}g(x),\;\lim_{x\to -\infty}g(x)\;\text{or}\;\lim_{x\to +\infty}g(x).$$ If $\lim g(x)=L$ and if the function $f$ is continuous at $L$, then $$\lim f(g(x))=f\!\left(\lim g(x)\right)=f(L).$$
إدخال النهاية داخل دالة خارجية متصلة.
4The continuity toolkitRemark 2.4.3
Remark 2.4.3Common special cases
If $\lim\limits_{x\to a}g(x)=L$, then (whenever expressions are defined):
  1. $\lim\limits_{x\to a}|g(x)|=|L|$,
  2. $\lim\limits_{x\to a}\sqrt{g(x)}=\sqrt{L}$ where $L>0$,
  3. $\lim\limits_{x\to a}\sin(g(x))=\sin(L)$,
  4. $\lim\limits_{x\to a}\cos(g(x))=\cos(L)$.
أربع حالات خاصة شائعة للنظرية السابقة.

Theorem 2.4.10Intermediate Value Theorem — IVT
Let $f$ be a continuous function on $[a,b]$. If $k$ is a number between $f(a)$ and $f(b)$, then there exists at least one $c\in[a,b]$ such that $f(c)=k$.

Let $f$ be a continuous function on $[a,b]$. If $f(a)f(b)<0$, then there exists $c\in(a,b)$ such that $f(c)=0$.
Remark 2.4.4
The cases where $f(a)=0$ or $f(b)=0$ are trivial.

For a function $f(x)$ to be continuous at $x=1$, it must satisfy three conditions:

  1. $f(1)$ is defined.
  2. $\lim\limits_{x\to 1}f(x)$ exists.
  3. $\lim\limits_{x\to 1}f(x)=f(1)$.
Analysis
  • The student mentioned Condition (1) and Condition (2).
  • However, the student forgot the third and most important condition that links the value of the function to its limit.
Missing condition $$\lim_{x\to 1}f(x)=f(1)$$
